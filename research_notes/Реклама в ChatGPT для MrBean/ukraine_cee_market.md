## 1. Exact availability for Ukraine (Ads Manager self-serve, restrictions, formats, language, surfaces)

### Takeaway
Ukraine was officially added on **21.10.2026** in the "26 new countries / 89 total" wave. Ukrainian advertisers can sign up through self-serve Ads Manager (ads.openai.com, with a Ukrainian-localized page) or through OpenAI's technology partners. Ads show only to logged-in adult users on the Free and Go plans. OpenAI has published nothing Ukraine-specific about how ads in the Ukrainian language, or in Ukrainian-language conversations, are handled. The standard global rules apply: some new accounts may target only their home country at first, and creative enhancements are on by default.

### Inferences
- A Ukrainian ФОП selling only within Ukraine is not hurt by the "home country first" rule, because Ukraine is the home country.
- Because creative enhancements can translate and rewrite ad text, a Ukrainian advertiser who wants to control Ukrainian versus Russian wording, or the brand voice, should check the opt-out setting before launch.
- The localized uk-UA advertiser site and help article suggest OpenAI treats Ukraine as a full self-serve market, not partner-only. Self-serve status of the ФОП legal form specifically is unconfirmed (see Q3).

## 2. Billing for Ukraine (currencies, Ukrainian cards, VAT, NBU limits, ФОП)

### Takeaway
No source confirms which billing currencies a Ukrainian ad account is offered. UAH is **not** shown in any published Ads minimum-budget table. The published minimums are $25/day (USD) and €15/day (EUR). Currency is locked at account creation. OpenAI has been a registered non-resident VAT payer in Ukraine since 12.11.2024. Ukrainian tax lawyers and accountants say a ФОП that is not a VAT payer owes no VAT on such e-services and may pay from its business card. NBU rules since 11.08.2026 let a ФОП key card pay abroad up to 400k UAH. I found no ChatGPT-Ads-specific reports of Monobank or PrivatBank card problems.

### Inferences
- For a Ukrainian ФОП, USD is the most likely account currency and the safest choice if offered, because all published Ads guidance and minimums are in USD/EUR. Verify on the currency selector before confirming, since the choice is permanent.
- A ФОП on simplified taxation (group 3, non-VAT payer) pays single tax on revenue, so ad spend is not a deductible expense. If OpenAI is still a registered VAT payer, no self-assessed VAT should arise. Whether OpenAI adds 20% VAT to Ads invoices for Ukrainian advertisers without a VAT ID is unconfirmed: expect the invoice to possibly include 20% on top of bids/budgets. That raises the effective minimum budget from $25 to about $30/day, an assumption to verify on the first invoice.
- The 400k UAH key-card limit is far above a test budget ($25/day × 14 days ≈ $350, plus the ~$100 hold), so NBU limits should not block a test.

### Gaps
- No official confirmation of currency options (USD/EUR/UAH) for Ukraine-registered ad accounts.
- No reports found, positive or negative, of Monobank, PrivatBank or other Ukrainian bank cards being accepted or declined for ChatGPT Ads specifically.
- No official OpenAI statement found on whether Ads invoices to Ukrainian advertisers carry Ukrainian VAT, or on the invoice format and legal entity (e.g. OpenAI Ireland versus OpenAI LLC) used for Ukrainian ad billing.
- The exact period of the 400k UAH limit (monthly?) was not shown in the snippets.

## 3. Business verification for Ukrainian entities (documents, problems)

### Takeaway
Verification runs inside Ads Manager, reportedly through Persona (identity plus business), and can take up to about 7 business days. I found no source describing which Ukrainian documents are accepted (витяг з ЄДР, Diia extract) or whether a ФОП, an individual entrepreneur rather than a company, passes business verification.
