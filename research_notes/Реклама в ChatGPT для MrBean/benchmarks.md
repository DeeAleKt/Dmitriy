## Q1. Reported CPM and CPC for ChatGPT ads: early US beta, self-serve era, changes over time, by market, October 2026 expansion

### Takeaway
Pricing has dropped sharply. The February 2026 US beta was sold at about $60 CPM with $200k+ commitments. By April, agency-reported CPMs were roughly $25–45 (some as low as $15), and CPC bidding arrived with a $3–5 recommended starting bid. Self-serve with no minimum opened to US businesses on 5 May 2026. Realized CPCs in published cases run about $0.44–$9.40 (mostly $1.7–5 in the US), and early EU e-commerce cases are well below $1. OpenAI publishes no CPM/CPC benchmarks or per-country price data. Ukraine became eligible on 21 Oct 2026, and no Ukrainian price data exists yet.

### Inferences
- The price path was roughly $60 CPM (Feb) → $25–45 CPM (Apr) → CPC era with US realized CPCs of about $1.7–5 (May onward). For CPM, "as low as $15" is the floor anecdote. The $60 still shown in Ads Manager is a pre-filled ceiling, not a price.
- Market differences are anecdotal, but the EU e-commerce cases (€0.44–0.78 CPC) sit well below US cases ($1.72–9.40). This fits fewer advertisers per market in newer, smaller auctions. One tracker explicitly says the data "does not show Europe is cheaper across the board", so the pattern is suggestive, not established.
- Ukraine joined on 21 Oct 2026 with an auction that is presumably thin, so early prices may be low. But OpenAI may apply reserve prices, and no Ukrainian data exists to confirm or rule this out.
- Minimum daily budgets appear to be set per market. India is ₹725/day, about $7.60 per one tracker, and Nigeria about $2.40/day. Ukrainian guides quote $25 or €15/day because UAH isn't supported. Treat the Ukraine minimum as unverified until checked in Ads Manager. At $25/day the floor is about $750/month, which is material for a small store.

### Gaps
- No OpenAI-published CPM/CPC averages for any market. Third-party guides say "OpenAI has said ChatGPT Ads has no performance benchmarks yet".
- No CPC/CPM data for Ukraine, Serbia, Poland, or any Eastern European or October-2026 market.
- No India, Canada, Australia, Latin America or UK CPC aggregate. The UK figure ($3.70) comes from one vendor.
- The pages behind #13, #16, #17 and #18 could not be identified or fetched. Verify on ppcone.cz and the agency blogs before citing.
- CPA bidding: announced as forthcoming in May. Its status by November 2026 was not confirmed, though Europe reportedly launched with "conversion-optimised campaigns".

## Q2. CTR, conversion rate, CPA/ROAS and AOV for ChatGPT ads, especially e-commerce, retail, home and furniture, vs search and display

### Takeaway
Panel CTRs are about 0.6–0.75% in English-speaking markets and 1.2–1.9% in Brazil, Mexico, Japan and South Korea. These are measured per ad-bearing exchange, so they can't be compared directly with Google CTR. Same-advertiser comparisons show ChatGPT CTR and especially conversion rates far below Google Search. The only large e-commerce dataset (1,898 shops) puts paid ChatGPT visit CVR at 0.31–0.37% vs 1.17% for Google paid search, and revenue per visit at $0.89 vs $2.45. Positive e-commerce results (ROAS 1.5x–4.7x) are single-advertiser, vendor-reported cases. The one furniture case (AFA Stores, 4.7x ROAS) is unverified.

### Inferences
- **Implied AOV (my arithmetic, all shops, not furniture-specific):** revenue per visit ÷ CVR = $0.89 / 0.0037 ≈ **$240 per converting ChatGPT ad visit** vs $2.45 / 0.0117 ≈ **$209 for Google paid search** (Kaiser & Schulze, US). Order values from ChatGPT clicks therefore look similar to or slightly higher than Google's. The gap is driven by conversion rate, not basket size.
- The best evidence (the 1,898-shop study plus every same-advertiser comparison) says a ChatGPT click is worth about one-third of a Google Search click for e-commerce. Positive ROAS results (1.49x, ~3.0x, 4.7x) are vendor-reported single cases that rely on platform attribution.
- For furniture specifically, conversion rates are already low on Google (2.7–3.0% search; 1–2.5% Shopping; see Q4). Applying the ChatGPT/Google ratio of about 0.32 (0.37/1.17) suggests a furniture ChatGPT CVR around **0.3–1%**, possibly lower. This is an extrapolation, not data.
- **Break-even CPC rule of thumb** for planning: max CPC ≈ AOV × gross margin × CVR. For example, a $300 AOV × 30% margin × 0.37% CVR ≈ **$0.33**. At a 1% CVR it is about $0.90. This is consistent with the paper's ~$0.26 break-even estimate.

- No display or Meta same-advertiser comparisons with ChatGPT ads were found.
- No CVR or ROAS from any Eastern European market.

## Q3. Industry benchmark reports that cover ChatGPT ads

### Takeaway
Three substantive third-party datasets exist: the Similarweb/Graphite panel (CTR, reach), the Adthena prompt tracking (placements, brands, categories), and the Kaiser & Schulze working paper (e-commerce CVR and revenue per visit). eMarketer offers forecasts. Skai and Tinuiti Q3 2026 reports did not include ChatGPT ad pricing in the snippets found, and no Smarter Ecommerce ChatGPT benchmark was found. None publish ChatGPT CPC/CPM benchmarks.

### Cited Findings
- **Similarweb + Graphite, "The State of ChatGPT Ads"** (28/29 Sep 2026): user-panel data from 9 live markets plus analysis of 98,000+ ads. It covers CTR per ad-bearing exchange, reach and the +112% CTR trend. Both firms have a commercial stake: Similarweb sells the tracking tool and Graphite is a growth agency — [Graphite](https://graphite.io/research/state-of-chatgpt-ads); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/chatgpt-ad-click-rate-gains-112-worldwide-similarweb-and-graphite-find/); [AirOps](https://www.airops.com/blog/chatgpt-ads-performance).
- **Adthena, Holiday AI Paid Search Report 2026** (released 4 Nov 2026): it tracked **204,489 ChatGPT ad placements** from June to September 2026 across the US, Australia and EU/UK. Apparel, consumer electronics and travel made up about 95% of placements, and Toys & Games advertisers grew 13x from June to August. It **does not measure clicks, spend or conversions** — [Adthena](https://www.adthena.com/ai-holiday-paid-search-report/); [Yahoo Finance/PR](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/adthena-report-shows-chatgpt-ads-140000553.html). Adthena's platform tracks about 300k daily prompts — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/chatgpt-ads-intelligence-adthenas-platform-tracks-300k-daily-prompts/). Another Adthena finding: "ChatGPT advertisers gain 65% as AI Overview queries shrink" — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/adthena-chatgpt-advertisers-gain-65-as-ai-overview-queries-shrink/). It also found a 39% retail share of ChatGPT ads on 24% of US queries — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/retail-took-39-of-chatgpt-ads-on-24-of-us-queries-adthena-finds/).
- **Digiday "Six thousand brands later…"** (early Nov 2026): Adthena tracked 1,082 distinct brands advertising on ChatGPT and projected about 6,830 for September from data through 20 Sept. The piece cites Kaiser & Schulze on conversion — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/in-graphic-detail-chatgpt-s-ad-growth-is-outrunning-the-proof-that-it-works/). This conflicts with OpenAI's claim of more than 100,000 advertisers — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-ads-expands-26-new-countries/). The definitions differ (brands observed in tracked prompts versus accounts that ran any campaign).
- **Kaiser (WU Vienna) & Schulze (Frankfurt School), "Can Advertising Fund Free AI? Evidence from ChatGPT's International Advertising Rollout"** (v1, Oct 2026, under review): 1,898 shops, with data from Grips Intelligence — [ecommerceresearch.org](https://ecommerceresearch.org/chatgpt-ads/); [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/11/05/study-finds-paid-chatgpt-ads-deliver-far-less-value-than/).
- **eMarketer:** the chatbot ad market (ChatGPT, Alexa+, Copilot, Gemini) is forecast at under $1B in 2026 and $5.41B by 2030 — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/chatgpt-ad-inventory-expands-attracts-big-brands-despite-basic-ad-tools-roas-questions). On that forecast OpenAI would miss its 2030 target of more than $100B by about 90% — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/openai-chatgpt-ads-100-billion-revenue-target-482365). eMarketer also forecasts US AI ad spend of $68B by 2030, with ChatGPT missing most of it — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/emarketer-says-us-ai-ad-spend-hits-68bn-by-2030-and-chatgpt-misses-most-of-it/). An eMarketer FAQ on formats and costs exists — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/faq-on-chatgpt-advertising--formats--costs--early-strategies-win).
- **Skai Q3 2026** (27 Oct 2026): covers Amazon, paid search and paid social. On Amazon, CPC fell 16% YoY and CVR fell 11% YoY. No ChatGPT pricing appeared in the snippet — [Skai](https://skai.io/press-releases/skai-q3-report-highlights-new-stakes-for-advertisers-as-ai-takes-on-more-media-decisions/).
- **Tinuiti:** only Google search click and spend changes surfaced, with no ChatGPT pricing — search synthesis [attribution approximate].
- **eMarketer revenue vs OpenAI run rate:** the "<$1B chatbot market in 2026" forecast conflicts with the reported $1B annualized ChatGPT ad run rate at 31 Aug 2026 — [media-marketing.com](https://www.media-marketing.com/en/news/advertising-in-chatgpt-has-reached-an-annual-revenue-run-rate-of-1-billion/). The two are not reconciled; an annualized run rate is not the same as calendar-year revenue.

### Inferences
- For planning, the Kaiser & Schulze CVR and revenue-per-visit numbers are the most decision-relevant benchmark for an e-commerce store: large sample, e-commerce only, multi-country. Similarweb/Graphite is the best reach and CTR reference but can't be turned into a Google-equivalent CTR.

### Gaps
- No Tinuiti, Skai or Smarter Ecommerce (smec) ChatGPT ads benchmark found. Sensor Tower and Statista ChatGPT ads data not found.
- No agency benchmark with CPC/CPM by vertical and sample sizes above a few dozen accounts.

## Q5. Purchasing-power / ad-price ratios US vs Ukraine for scaling ChatGPT ad expectations (estimate method)

### Takeaway
Meta CPMs in Ukraine are about 1/7 to 1/10 of the US level (about $2.12 vs $16–23). Google CPCs for Ukrainian e-commerce are about 1/10 to 1/50 of US furniture search CPC. Scaling US ChatGPT prices by those ratios gives a **hypothetical** Ukrainian ChatGPT range of about $1.5–6.5 CPM and $0.15–0.70 CPC. This is a ratio-based estimate, not data: the ChatGPT auction in Ukraine is brand-new, and any OpenAI reserve prices or minimum budgets could keep it from scaling down this way.

### Cited Findings
- US Meta CPM: median **$16.08** for e-commerce, the most expensive of 52 markets — [Lebesgue](https://lebesgue.io/facebook-ads/facebook-cpm-by-country). AdKit's Sept 2026 data from 800+ advertisers gives a US median of **$22.75** — [AdKit](https://adkit.so/resources/how-much-do-facebook-ads-cost). Adligator gives a typical US range of $16–21 — [Adligator](https://adligator.com/blog/meta-ads-cpm-by-country-benchmarks).
- Ukraine Meta CPM: **$1.50–3.00** — [Adligator](https://adligator.com/blog/meta-ads-cpm-by-country-benchmarks). AdKit gives a median of **$2.12** and CPC of about $0.06–0.37 — [AdKit](https://adkit.so/resources/how-much-do-facebook-ads-cost). Adligator says US CPMs are "roughly 10–15x higher than developing markets" — [Adligator](https://adligator.com/blog/meta-ads-cpm-by-country-benchmarks).
- The US Meta median rose 22% from Oct to Nov 2025, so Q4 seasonality matters — [AdKit](https://adkit.so/resources/how-much-do-facebook-ads-cost).
- US ChatGPT inputs: CPM about $15–45 (Q1 #3, #10); CPC $1.72 (Opascope), $3–5 recommended start bid, about $3–3.50 agency average (Q1 #6, #11, #16).
- EU e-commerce ChatGPT CPC: €0.44 (NL/DE/AT/FR) — [PromptMarketing](https://www.promptmarketing.com/about/cases/chatgpt-ads-ecommerce/). DACH: €0.54–0.78 (Q1 #13, attribution approximate).
- Ukraine Google CPC: home goods and e-commerce at about $0.07–0.37 (Q4 U2).

### Inferences
**Estimate method (clearly an estimate, not observed data):**
1. **Meta CPM ratio method.** US/UA ratio = 16.08/2.12 ≈ 7.6x up to 22.75/2.12 ≈ 10.7x; Adligator ranges give about 5–14x. Dividing US ChatGPT CPM ($15–45) by 7.6–10.7 gives **≈ $1.4–5.9 CPM**. Dividing US ChatGPT CPC ($1.72–5) gives **≈ $0.16–0.66 CPC**.
2. **Google CPC ratio method.** US furniture search CPC $3.97 vs UA e-commerce/home $0.07–0.37 gives a ratio of about 11–57x. Applied to US ChatGPT CPC ($1.72–5), that gives **≈ $0.03–0.45**. This is a lower bound, because the ChatGPT auction may have floors.
3. **Observed nearest-market anchor.** EU e-commerce ChatGPT CPCs of €0.44–0.78 in Western Europe. Eastern Europe is plausibly at or below that, so a working range of **$0.20–0.70 CPC** is reasonable for planning. This is an assumption.
4. **Illustrative budget math, not a forecast.** If the minimum is $25/day (unverified for Ukraine): $25 ÷ $0.30–0.70 CPC = **~35–80 clicks/day**.
   - At the paid-ChatGPT CVR of 0.31–0.37% (Kaiser & Schulze), that is **~0.1–0.3 orders/day (≈3–9/month)**.
   - At an optimistic 1–2.35% CVR (Opascope-level), it is **~0.4–1.9 orders/day**.
   - With a furniture AOV of A, ROAS ≈ (orders × A) / spend. A low-CVR scenario needs a high AOV and margin to break even. For example, 6 orders/month × $300 AOV = $1,800 revenue on $750 spend ≈ 2.4x ROAS before margin.
5. Purchasing power: lower Ukrainian AOVs (in USD terms) reduce the break-even CPC proportionally (Q2 formula: max CPC ≈ AOV × margin × CVR). So even a cheaper Ukrainian ChatGPT auction may not beat Google Shopping/PMax in Ukraine, where CPCs are only a few UAH.

### Gaps
- No observed Ukrainian ChatGPT CPC/CPM, so the method cannot be validated. Revisit after 4–8 weeks of Ukrainian campaign data. Agencies such as Advermedia, Spilno and Holostenko are positioned to publish first.
- No published reserve-price or floor policy from OpenAI by country.
- No Google Ads CPM ratio data for US vs Ukraine found; only Meta CPM and Google CPC ranges.
- Exchange-rate assumptions inside the Ukrainian sources (UAH→USD) were not verified.

## Q6. ChatGPT user base: global WAU, free/Go vs paid, Ukraine usage, how many users see ads

### Takeaway
ChatGPT has roughly 0.9–1.2B weekly users. The 900M figure (Feb 2026) is confirmed; about 1B (July 2026) is reported from an OpenAI spokesperson; 1.2B (Nov 2026) comes from an unverified blog. OpenAI's last confirmed paid count is about 50M subscribers. Ads show only to Free and Go users, so well over 90% of weekly users are ad-eligible, and by Sept 2026 roughly 28–46% of eligible users in panel markets had seen at least one ad. In Ukraine chatgpt.com ranks #7 among all websites (Sept 2026), against an internet population of about 35.3M. No Ukraine-specific ChatGPT user count was found.

### Cited Findings
- 900M weekly active users as of 27 Feb 2026 — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/27/chatgpt-reaches-900m-weekly-active-users); [The Keyword](https://www.thekeyword.co/news/chatgpt-hits-900-million-weekly-active-users). There were 800M in Oct 2025 — [WiserNotify](https://wisernotify.com/blog/chatgpt-users/) [attribution approximate].
- About 1B WAU in July 2026, per an OpenAI spokesperson to The Verge; one article says more than 1B was confirmed on 31 July 2026 — [GetAIRefs](https://getairefs.com/blog/chatgpt-user-statistics/); [WiserNotify](https://wisernotify.com/blog/chatgpt-users/) [attribution approximate].
- 1.2B WAU as of 3 Nov 2026, with about 112M ChatGPT Go subscribers making up about 92% of paid users. This comes from a single blog and is **unverified**. It also implies about 122M paid users, which conflicts with the 50M official figure — [Apiyi blog](https://help.apiyi.com/en/openai-consumer-users-enterprise-growth-flywheel-en.html).
- 50M paying consumer subscribers, first disclosed in April 2026 (some sources date it to Feb 2026) — [Scalevise](https://scalevise.com/resources/chatgpt-50m-consumer-subscribers-paid-ai-demand/).
- Estimates put free users at about 81% of active users, while another says only about 5% pay — [Second Talent](https://www.secondtalent.com/resources/chatgpt-statistics/); [Omnibound](https://www.omnibound.ai/blog/chatgpt-user-statistics) [attribution approximate]. ChatGPT Go launched worldwide at $8/month — same sources [attribution approximate].
- Ads are shown only on the Free and Go plans — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/openai-chatgpt-ads-26-countries.html); [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-ads-expands-26-new-countries/).
- By Sept 2026, 27.8% (Mexico) to 45.7% (UK) of ad-eligible users had seen at least one ad — [Similarweb](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/chatgpt-ads-ctr-by-country/).
- ChatGPT's share of gen-AI website visits fell from 71.8% (Oct 2025) to 53.7% (Apr 2026), and about 56.4% in Sept 2026, as Gemini grew — [Similarweb on X](https://x.com/Similarweb/status/2109561054883717264).
- **Ukraine:** chatgpt.com ranks **#7** in Similarweb's top websites in Ukraine (Sept 2026) — [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/top-websites/ukraine/). Ukraine had **35.3M internet users** at end-2025 (89.6% of the population) and 23M social media identities (58.5%). YouTube reaches 23M (65.2% of internet users), TikTok 19M adults, Facebook 14.2M and Instagram 12.6M — [Mezha (Digital 2026 data)](https://mezha.ua/en/articles/yakim-bude-internet-ta-cifrova-ekonomika-u-2026-roci-307553/); [DataReportal Ukraine](https://datareportal.com/digital-in-ukraine); [SalesDoubler](https://salesdoubler.pro/en/digital-trends/).

### Inferences
- If roughly 50M of 0.9–1.0B weekly users pay and Go users still see ads, the ad-eligible share is about 95% or more of WAU (my arithmetic, using the official 50M).
- In Ukraine, a #7 site rank suggests ChatGPT is a mass-reach property, likely millions of monthly users. The audience actually typing furniture-purchase prompts in Ukrainian or Russian, and therefore eligible for a furniture ad, is a small subset. Ad reach in new markets also builds over weeks: panel markets went from 0% to 28–46% exposure over 5–25 weeks.

### Gaps
- No Similarweb or DataReportal figure for ChatGPT users or visits in Ukraine. The country split on Similarweb's chatgpt.com page was zeroed out in search results.
- No official current WAU (Oct/Nov 2026) or Free/Go/Plus split from OpenAI.
- No data on what share of ChatGPT prompts in Ukraine are commercial or furniture-related, or on ad load frequency (ads per ad-eligible response) by market.
