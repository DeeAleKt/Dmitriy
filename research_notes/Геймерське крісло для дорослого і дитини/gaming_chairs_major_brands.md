# Gaming (racing-style) chairs from established gaming/peripheral brands sold new in Ukraine for ≤6,000 UAH (mid-October 2026)

## 1. Which models are currently sold in Ukraine at ≤6,000 UAH (regular or promo)?

### Takeaway
By mid-October 2026 most mainstream gaming-brand lines cost more than 6,000 UAH in Ukraine. That includes 2E Bushido II/Hibagon II/Basan II/Ogama II, Cougar Armor One/Fusion/Explore, HATOR Sport Essential/Darkside/Arc, Genesis Nitro 440 G2, Lorgar, Canyon, GamePro, MSI MAG, Gamemax GCR09, Arozzi, Anda Seat and DXRacer. Only a small group could be confirmed at or under 6,000 UAH:
- HATOR Flash (HTC-401/HTC-400)
- Trust GXT 703 Riye Black
- Xtrike ME GC-905
- Huzaro Force 4.7 (some colours and shops)
- 2E Gaming HEBI (some shops; partly discontinued)
- Aerocool Baron Iron Black / AC120 (Brain; stock not verified)
- Defender Watcher (lowest offers only)

Trust GXT 704 Ravy may also qualify, but its price data is old.

### Inferences
- Above-budget brands may still dip near 6,000 UAH in short promos, but none was seen at or under 6,500 in October 2026. Cougar Fusion S at 6,799 (KTC) is the closest. So no exception for chairs up to 6,500 UAH is justified for Cougar, 2E (except HEBI), HATOR (except Flash), Genesis, MSI or Lorgar.
- For this budget, the realistic established-brand choice is: Trust GXT 703 Riye, Xtrike ME GC-905, HATOR Flash, Huzaro Force 4.7, 2E HEBI, Aerocool Baron/AC120 (if actually in stock), the lowest Defender Watcher offers, and possibly Trust GXT 704 Ravy.

All prices and specs therefore come from search-engine page snapshots (summaries of pages at index time), not live pages. Prices are "seen 2026-10-16" in those snapshots, but page dates were mostly not shown. Some pages were clearly dated: Comfy Xtrike page updated Oct 2026; Telemart Lorgar Sept 2026; Foxtrot's 2E table June 2025; a Moyo Trust page 2023; ek.ua pages often years old.

## 5. Fit assessment: 185 cm / 80–100 kg adult and a 9-year-old (~130–140 cm, ~28–35 kg)

### Takeaway
No in-budget chair fits both users well out of the box.

## 6. Best 2–4 price/quality picks for this household

### Takeaway
1. **Trust GXT 703 Riye (Black, 5,683–5,999 UAH)**: the best-specified in-budget chair for a 185 cm / 100 kg adult (140 kg, class 4, 78 cm backrest, 54×52 cm seat). Its flip-up armrests also suit a child.
2. **Xtrike ME GC-905 (4,899–5,199 UAH)**: the cheapest chair rated 135–150 kg, with a 44–54 cm seat-height range. It has no user reviews, so buy with a 14-day return option.

2E HEBI should be excluded for this household (100 kg / 175 cm maximum). Huzaro Force 4.7 is better suited to the child than to the adult and has recurring quality-control complaints.
