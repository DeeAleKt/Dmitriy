# Verification of the ≤6,000 UAH gaming-chair shortlist and the brands the first round missed (Ukraine, mid-October 2026)

Method note for the report writer:
- **How the data was gathered.** Direct page fetching of Ukrainian retailer and brand sites is blocked in this environment, so every figure below comes from search-engine extracts retrieved on **16 Oct 2026**. "Seen 16 Oct 2026" means the price was in the search index that day; most extracts do not show when the page itself was captured.
- **Date markers that were visible:**
  - Rozetka extracts carrying the "ДНІ ROZETKA 6–12 жовтня" banner were captured in October 2026.
  - A Moyo extract showing pickup "21.10" was captured in October 2026.
  - Comfy's GT Racer list is dated August 2026.
- **Scope.** The two earlier notes (`gaming_chairs_major_brands.md`, `gaming_chairs_budget_local_brands.md`) are not repeated. This file confirms, corrects or extends them, and figures carried over from them are marked "(prior round)".
- **Search budget.** 60 web searches were used, the limit for this task.
- **Out of scope.** Ergonomic mesh chairs are covered by another researcher.

## 1. Shortlist status: which shortlisted models are really sold new at ≤6,000 UAH right now?

### Takeaway
**Confirmed at or under 6,000 UAH at first-party chains:**
- **Trust GXT 703 Riye, Black version only:** 5,683–5,999 UAH. Every other colour is 6,719 UAH or more.
- **Xtrike ME GC-905:** 4,899–5,599 UAH.
- **HATOR Flash HTC-401:** 4,399 UAH promo.
- **AMF VR Racer Dexter Vector:** 5,499–5,799 UAH.

**Possibly in budget, but stock or page date not verified:**
- Aerocool Baron Iron Black (Brain, 5,791 UAH)
- Special4You ExtremeRace E5623, the plain series (Foxtrot, 4,299 UAH)

**No longer in budget, or not sold new:**
- Trust GXT 704 Ravy: discontinued, no Ukrainian offer.
- Special4You ExtremeRace 3: now 6,799–8,714 UAH.
- Huzaro Force 4.7: Rozetka 7,199 UAH.
- GT Racer X-2534-F: lowest 6,150 UAH.
- Defender Watcher: 5,969 UAH and up, mostly 6,070 or more.
- RZTK: the only RZTK model rated ≥130 kg that is still sold is the **Cobra, at 6,299 UAH** (just over the cap).

### Inferences
- **Trust GXT 703 Riye only qualifies in Black, and only just.** If the Telemart/KTC promo ends (old price 6,799), Artline's 5,683 is the only in-cap offer left. Treat it as "in budget while the promo lasts".
- **HATOR Flash at 4,399 looks like a long-running promo.** It was seen at Telemart, Artline and Rozetka in both research rounds.
- **The Xtrike GC-905 price depends on the seller.** First-party chains (Comfy, Foxtrot, ITbox) charge 4,899–5,599, while marketplace sellers on Allo and Rozetka ask 6,069–8,360. Buy from the chain.
- **AMF Dexter Vector's 5,499 is the brand's own price.** It appears at amf.ua and Rozetka, so it is less promo-dependent than the Trust.

### Gaps
- Live stock could not be confirmed for any offer. This includes Brain's Aerocool Baron Iron Black at 5,791, which may be old stock of a 2020-era model, and the Foxtrot E5623 page date.
- Price history was not available, so it could not be shown whether RZTK Cobra or GT Racer X-2534-F regularly drop below 6,000.
- A "Trust GXT 704 Roniq RGB" name appeared in one non-Ukrainian result ([listing](https://aspire-payments.uws.ac.uk/product/BEV-GWP-TRU-25979/trust-gxt-704-roniq-rgb-gaming-chair-the-end)). It was not investigated.

## 2. Missed brands: does 1stPlayer or another missed brand offer a better option ≤6,000 UAH?

### Takeaway
**No.** None of the brands missed in the first round offers a better chair at or under 6,000 UAH for this household.
- **1stPlayer**
  - Its only in-budget models, **S01 (~5,299)** and **P01 (~5,199, mesh back)**, are rated 120 kg with a class 3 gas lift. On paper they are weaker for the 100 kg adult than Trust GXT 703, AMF Vector or Xtrike GC-905.
  - Its strong models are rated 160 kg (FK2, DK2, WIN101, BD1, DK1 Pro), but they cost 6,999–15,280 UAH and most are out of stock.
  - FK2 at 6,999 even pairs its "160 kg" with a class 3 lift and a nylon base.
- **Lorgar, Canyon and GamePro:** no Ukrainian offer at or under 6,000. The cheapest found was GamePro GC575 at 6,999.
- **"Gamery GX-150":** the only matching chair is the **Kruger&Matz Warrior GX-150** (150 kg). No Ukrainian offer was found.
- **Sandberg:** the only sub-6,000 model is a mesh-back "ErgoFusion Gaming Chair Basic", which is outside this scope.
- **Other 150 kg / class 4 / metal-base chairs that surfaced:**
  - GameMax GCR08: discontinued.
  - GT Racer X-3101: 6,999, and its base is nylon, not metal.
  - Lorgar Ace 422: over budget, out of stock.
  - Canyon Fobos and Deimos: no Ukrainian price found for Fobos; Deimos costs 7,499 UAH or more (prior round).

## 6. Ranked verdict: the 2–3 gaming chairs that are truly the best price/quality for this household

### Takeaway
1. **Trust GXT 703 Riye, Black (25128)**
   - Price: 5,683 UAH at Artline; 5,999 at Telemart/KTC (promo from 6,799).
   - Why: the only in-budget chair whose **class 4 gas lift and 140 kg rating** are documented consistently. It also has a 78 cm back, flip-up armrests, a tilt lock, an established brand and 12 months' Ukrainian warranty.
   - Weak points: other colours are over budget; no Ukrainian reviews; the minimum seat height is unpublished.
2. **AMF VR Racer Dexter Vector**
   - Price: 5,499 UAH at amf.ua and Rozetka.
   - Why: 150 kg, steel frame, 80 cm back, and the **lowest documented minimum seat height (43 cm)** of the 140–150 kg chairs. It is the only in-budget chair with a **185 cm / 100 kg owner endorsement**, and it is made by a domestic manufacturer.
   - Before buying: confirm the gas-lift class. Expect squeaks, and note that the adult sits at the top of its 160–185 cm range.
   - **Tie-breaker:** if the seller confirms class 4, it is equal or better value than the Trust.
3. **Xtrike ME GC-905**
   - Price: 4,899 UAH at Comfy (GC-905BK); 5,199 at Foxtrot and ITbox.
   - Why: the cheapest chair rated 135–150 kg, with a 44–54 cm seat range and pillows.
   - Weak points: no user reviews anywhere; the gas-lift class is unknown; listings mention both 50 mm and 100 mm strokes. Buy only the 100 mm configuration, and only with a 14-day return.

**Stretch, over the cap:** RZTK Cobra at 6,299 UAH, and only if a Rozetka promo brings it to 6,000 or less. It has the best paper spec near the budget (metal base, class 4, 150 kg, moulded foam, 170–190 cm). - **HATOR Flash:** cheap and child-friendly, but its load rating may be only 100 kg, with a 71 cm back and fixed armrests.
- **Defender Watcher:** 2.7/5, 120 kg, 6-month warranty.

### Inferences
- **Ranking logic.**
  - The adult's safety comes first: a documented gas-lift class and load margin, because gas lifts sinking around 90 kg is a recurring budget-tier complaint (prior round).
  - Next comes the adult's fit (backrest, top seat height), then the child's minimum seat height and seat depth, then the user evidence, then price.
  - Trust 703 wins on the first criterion. AMF Vector wins on the child's fit and on user evidence. Xtrike wins on price.
- **Trust vs AMF is close.** The order flips if the household weights the child's fit and a ~200–500 UAH saving over documented gas-lift class, or once AMF confirms class 4 for the Vector.
- **Before buying any of the three:**
  - Check the exact SKU and colour (Trust: Black only; Xtrike: the 100 mm stroke).
  - Ask for the gas-lift class (AMF, Xtrike).
  - Buy from a first-party chain with a 14-day return, so both users can test it at home.
  - Budget for a child footrest and a back cushion.
- **Stretching the budget.** If the budget can stretch about 300 UAH, RZTK Cobra at 6,299 is the only chair here that meets every "hardware" criterion (metal base, class 4, 150 kg). Its review record is why it is not ranked above the in-cap picks.
