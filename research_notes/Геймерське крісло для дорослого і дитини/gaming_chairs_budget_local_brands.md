# Budget / mass-market / Ukrainian & Polish-brand gaming (racing-style) chairs ≤6,000 UAH in Ukraine (mid-October 2026)

Method note for the report writer: direct page fetching (WebFetch/curl) was blocked for every Ukrainian retail domain in this environment (DNS failure / proxy 403). All data below therefore come from search-engine extracts of retailer, brand and catalogue pages, retrieved 16 Oct 2026. Most extracts show **no snapshot date**: treat prices as "last indexed", not as verified on 16 Oct 2026. The only dated price block is Comfy's GT Racer listing, which the extract dates to **August 2026**. The session's shared web-search quota (200 calls) ran out mid-research, so several brands are only partly covered (see Gaps). "n/d" = snapshot date not shown.

## 1. Which gaming models from these brands are sold in Ukraine at ≤6,000 UAH, and how do they classify (decent vs junk)?

### Takeaway
Under 6,000 UAH the segment splits in two:
- **Junk tier, ~2,700–4,500 UAH.** Bonro, DIEGO, Hanert, DEXON, RZTK, Barsky Sportdrive, and the GT Racer X-23xx fabric models. Most are rated for 80–120 kg and sized for users up to about 170–175 cm.
- **Thin "decent" tier, ~5,000–6,000 UAH.** It holds only a few 150 kg-rated models: AMF VR Racer Dexter Vector, some Special4You ExtremeRace versions, and the GT Racer X-2534-F, which usually sits just above the cap.

Several named brands had no gaming chairs at all, or none within budget: Richman, Nowy Styl, Kreslalux, FragON, and Halmar Hamlet.

### Inferences
- **Decent for this household (150 kg-class, adult-sized, ≤~6,000):** AMF VR Racer Dexter Vector and Special4You ExtremeRace / ExtremeRace 3 (150 kg). GT Racer X-2534-F also qualifies, but it is over the cap, with a 6,150 UAH floor on Hotline.
- **Adequate only for a light or short user ("junk" for this household):**
  - GT Racer X-2324 / X-2339: 80 kg, ≤175 cm, wood frame
  - Barsky Sportdrive Game: plastic frame and base, ≤170 cm
  - Bonro range
  - RZTK Viper: 100 kg
  - Hanert G-Force: 100–120 kg
  - DIEGO
  - DEXON RD-6017: 120 kg, sparse evidence
  - Special4You Riko: 120 kg, plastic base
  - AMF Dexter Frenzy / Djaks: 120 kg
  - AKLAS Hornet: 120 kg
- Polish furniture brands (Halmar, Signal) look marginal in Ukrainian retail now: obsolete or unpriced listings, and the one priced Halmar is over budget.

## 2. Prices at several stores, Hotline ranges, stock, warranty and service

### Takeaway
Prices for the same model vary by up to 2× between stores, because marketplace sellers mark up heavily. Stock is patchy: many ExtremeRace, Barsky and GT Racer variants show out of stock. Warranty is almost always 12 months; Barsky claims 5 years. GT Racer and AMF have the most visible Ukrainian service infrastructure, as brand-owned stores and warehouses.

## 5. Real-user evidence (ratings, review counts, recurring complaints and praise; tall/heavy users; children)

Recurring complaints across the budget tier:
- Peeling or cracking eco-leather within months to 2 years
- Squeaks
- Gas lifts that sink under ~90 kg users

## 6. Fit assessment: 185 cm / 80–100 kg adult and a ~9-year-old (130–140 cm, 28–35 kg)

### Takeaway
The adult is the binding constraint. Load, backrest height and recommended user height rule out every sub-4,500 UAH model found. Among 150 kg models, **AMF Dexter Vector** has the best two-user compromise: 43–53 cm seat height, so the lowest minimum among 150 kg chairs. **GT Racer X-2534-F** fits the adult best (≤190 cm, 82 cm back, metal frame), but its 52 cm minimum seat height is poor for the child. Every racing seat found is deeper (46–52 cm) and higher (≥42 cm) than suits a 130–140 cm child, so the child will need a footrest and a back cushion whatever chair is chosen.

## 7. Which 2–4 models in this group are the best price/quality for this household, and why?

### Takeaway
Ranked picks:
1. **AMF VR Racer Dexter Vector** (~5,499 UAH, amf.ua): best in-budget compromise.
2. **Special4You ExtremeRace** in a 150 kg version priced ≤6,000 (ExtremeRace 3 seen at ~5,459 at Epicentr): conditional pick.
3. **GT Racer X-2534-F Fabric**: adult-first stretch pick, **over the cap** at 6,150–7,819 on Hotline, with no evidence of regular sub-6,000 discounts.

Everything cheaper in this segment is rated 80–120 kg and/or ≤170–175 cm and should be treated as unsuitable for the 185 cm / up-to-100 kg adult.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **Pick 1, AMF VR Racer Dexter Vector: best balance for *both* users within the cap.**
  - It is the only in-budget 150 kg model whose seat goes down to 43 cm, which helps the child.
  - It has a direct tall/heavy user endorsement.
  - It comes from a domestic manufacturer, which helps warranty and parts.
  - Before buying: confirm the gas-lift class (ask for class 3–4) and stock at amf.ua. Expect possible squeaks; add a footrest and lumbar cushion for the child.
- **Pick 2, Special4You ExtremeRace (150 kg version at ≤6,000): conditional.**
  - Good load rating; the class 4 / 4D spec is documented on E7139.
  - Risks: the in-budget ExtremeRace 3 is listed with a "plastic frame", its 51 cm seat width may be narrow for 100 kg, PU peeling is reported, and stock and prices are erratic.
  - Buy only after confirming frame material and gas-lift class for the exact SKU, and prefer a fabric version if one exists.
- **Pick 3 (over cap), GT Racer X-2534-F Fabric: best for the adult, worst for the child.**
  - Worth it only if found ≤6,000–6,200 UAH. No evidence was found that it is regularly discounted below 6,000.
  - Budget for a tall footrest for the child.
- **Not recommended for sharing, but cheap child-friendly fallback: GT Racer X-2339 Fabric** (~4,000–4,200 UAH). Its 42 cm minimum seat, 46 cm depth, footrest and fabric suit the child, but the manufacturer-side 80 kg / 175 cm limits make it wrong for the 185 cm / 80–100 kg adult.
- If the household must share and the budget is firm, the evidence points to the AMF Vector as the safe in-cap choice. Otherwise, consider models covered by the other researchers (established gaming brands or ergonomic chairs), since this local/budget group offers few 150 kg-class chairs under 6,000 UAH.

- Not found: AMF Vector's gas-lift class, foam type and frame material; ExtremeRace 3's true frame material; X-2534-F price history.
- Halmar and Signal chairs might offer a sub-6,000 alternative, but no Ukrainian offers could be found before the search quota ran out.
